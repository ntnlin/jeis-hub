## Strategy Framework

When Jei asks about project direction, run through:

**Vision:** What problem does this solve and for whom? How is the world different if it succeeds?

**Business model:** What does the user "buy"? Revenue streams? If marketplace: what are both sides?

**Competitor analysis:**
- Who are direct + indirect competitors?
- What have they done well? What's their acquisition strategy?
- What can we do better?

**Narrative:** Every narrative in crypto needs a good story. Interweave Jei's unique edge with the project's unique edge. Simple summary under 3 sentences.

**Community + marketing channels:** Discord, X, Telegram, physical events — how do they work together?

**User acquisition stages:** 0→100, 100→1,000, 1,000+. Different strategies per stage.

## Fundraising Framework

### Investor types
- VCs: thesis-driven, need narrative + traction + team
- Angels: personal bet on founder, relationship-driven
- DAOs/grants: ecosystem alignment, community fit

### Round structure signals
- Pre-seed: proof of concept + team, <$2M
- Seed: product + early traction, $2-5M
- Series A: PMF + growth metrics, $5M+

### Fundraising materials checklist
- [ ] Pitch deck (10-12 slides max)
- [ ] Strategy document (internal — forces clarity)
- [ ] One-pager / teaser
- [ ] Data room (cap table, financials, roadmap)
- [ ] Demo video or live demo

### Fundraising process
1. Build warm intro pipeline (not cold outreach)
2. First 3 investors set the terms — be selective
3. Create FOMO with parallel processes
4. Move fast: decision in 2 weeks or pass

## Pitching Framework

**Opening:** What is it? Who is it for? Why now?

**Problem:** Make the audience feel the pain. Specifics beat generics.

**Solution:** How you solve it, simply. One clear mechanism.

**Traction:** Real numbers. Fake or projected numbers kill credibility.

**Team:** Why YOU for this problem? Specific unfair advantages.

**Ask:** Specific amount, specific use of funds, specific milestone.

**Common pitching mistakes:**
- Too much technical detail (save for Q&A)
- Vague market size ("$100B TAM")
- No clear narrative thread
- Weak problem definition
- Underemphasizing team

## Community Building Framework

### Philosophy
Community ≠ audience. Community = people with shared identity.

### Building stages
1. **0-100:** Manually onboard. Know every member. Create founding member identity.
2. **100-1,000:** Give members tools to bring others. Referral mechanics. Reward early contributors.
3. **1,000+:** Governance, sub-communities, events. Community runs itself.

### Channel strategy
- **X:** Founder presence, thought leadership, building in public
- **Telegram:** Real-time community, fast feedback loops
- **Discord:** Deep engagement, structured communities, voice
- **Events:** IRL = trust + retention multiplier

### Founder content rule
People follow people, not companies. Jei should post on @jeivyou as Jei, not as company account. Personal = authentic = compounds.

## Design Principles (for product decisions)

- UX designers: flows and usability
- UI designers: interface and design system
- Brand designers: identity (usually contractor, short-term)
- Product designer: end-to-end product strategy (most valuable hire at early stage)

Web3 design trap: building for Web3 natives instead of the next 100M users.
