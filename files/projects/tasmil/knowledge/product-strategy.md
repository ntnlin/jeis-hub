# Tasmil Finance — Product Strategy & Growth Direction

**Source:** Jei's internal strategy document  
**Last updated:** 2026-05-19  
**Confidence:** 100% (first-party document)  
**RULE: DeFAI ONLY — NOT RWA**

---

## What Tasmil Is

**DeFAI execution layer.** Users deploy capital with a single command.

Users say: *"Put my money to work."*

Tasmil automatically: bridges, swaps, routes to best yield.

Users do NOT need to understand: chains, protocols, or pools.

---

## Problem It Solves

DeFi is fragmented. To switch chains, users must:
- Research multiple protocols
- Navigate wallets, DEXs, DeFi platforms, pools
- Manually bridge, swap, choose pools

Result: high friction, users explore but don't execute (don't deploy capital). Web2 users can't access DeFi at all.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Strategy Layer (Builder-driven growth)
- Builders create yield strategies (auto-rebalance, portfolio logic)
- Strategies publicly show positions and PnL
- Users: browse → select → copy strategy (no DeFi knowledge needed)
- Reviews + ratings for builders
- High revenue share for builders → strong incentive for quality
- Builders become the growth engine

### 2. One-line Cross-chain Routing
- Example: "Put 1,000 USDC into the safest high-yield pool"
- System auto-bridges, swaps, routes to best pool
- Tasmil controls capital flow, earns fees from routing/swaps/execution

### 3. AI Execution Layer
- AI minimizes command count (Claude-style: combine actions)
- Understands intent + goals (yield vs safety)
- Classifies risk, filters pools, optimizes routing
- Explains in simple non-technical language
- Pushes users to execute — NOT a chat-only AI
- Focus: capital flow and real usage

---

## UX Principle

**One unified chat interface.** User opens app → lands in chat → no need to choose anything first.

Mental model: like depositing money into a bank, but lending XLM/stablecoins to earn higher yield.

No complex dashboards. No agent selection. Minimal steps.

---

## Vision

Tasmil = default entry point to DeFi.
- Web2 users: deposit and earn, no DeFi knowledge
- Crypto users: no need to research protocols
- DeFi does not lack yield. It lacks execution. Tasmil solves execution.

## Roadmap & KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Waitlist Wallets | 100–200 (M1 only) |
| Active Depositors | 500–1,000 |

| Routing Volume | $10K–$20K |
| TVL | $5K–$10K |

**P1 Growth tactics:**
- Email drip (3 emails / 2 weeks): scarcity + CTA
- 2-3 educational threads/week on X: DeFi yield basics, cross-chain pain points
- 3-5 micro-influencers (5K-20K followers): early access + honest reviews
- Focus on 2 use cases: stablecoin yield + single-click cross-chain
- Retention: weekly email ("Your yield this week: +$X"), push notifications

## Content & Marketing Rules

**DO talk about:**
- AI-powered execution (DeFAI)
- Cross-chain routing in one command
- Strategy marketplace and builder performance
- Yield without complexity
- Stellar DeFi ecosystem
- Autonomous finance agents

**DO NOT talk about:**
- RWA (real-world assets) — flagged and blocked
- Asset tokenization
- Real-world asset tokenization
- Asset-backed anything

**Content angle:** Execution problem, not information problem. Most DeFi content talks about yields — Tasmil talks about getting people to actually use them.
